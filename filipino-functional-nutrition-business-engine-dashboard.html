Business Engine (Deploy & Create Value)

Dashboard for roadmap, tasking, reporting, and weekly execution.

This page is designed to help us run the business with lean and agile discipline. The goal is not only to have ideas, but to see what phase we are in, who owns what, what the next deliverables are, what is blocked, what is being validated, and what should be reported every week.

Cadence Weekly sprint review, biweekly roadmap check, monthly metrics review.
Primary Decision Lens Customer validation first, then system stability, then scale readiness.
Core Output Clear owners, visible progress, useful reporting, and faster decision-making.

Operating Foundations

This dashboard breaks the business into executive layers: phase, team, sprint work, reporting, and decision logs. Each layer should answer one question clearly.

Phase

What stage are we in right now, and what proves we are ready to move forward?

Team

Who owns each function, and what are they responsible for this week?

Sprint Work

What is now, next, blocked, and validated in the current sprint?

Reporting

What metrics moved, what was decided, and what needs escalation?

How to use this dashboard properly
Use the phase view for direction, the team view for ownership, the sprint board for execution, and the reporting section for decision-making. Do not mix strategic ideas, tasks, and metrics into one messy list.
What should be updated weekly
  • Top priorities for the week
  • New blockers or risks
  • Customer feedback and insights
  • Metrics snapshot
  • Key decisions made

Phase Roadmap

Each phase should have a narrow objective, a clear validation question, and a gate before expansion.

Phase 1 — Validate MVP

Goal: prove demand, identify winning flavors, and confirm repeat purchase behavior.

  • Small-batch flavor testing
  • Hospital, gym, and Filipino community pilots
  • Collect structured customer feedback
  • Track repeat purchase signals
  • Decide which hero SKUs move forward
Phase 2 — Systemize & Grow

Goal: build repeatable systems so growth does not break quality or operations.

  • Create SOPs and handoff workflows
  • Stabilize supply chain and inventory
  • Launch Shopify, Amazon, or direct ordering
  • Expand creator and community marketing
  • Track monthly revenue and operational stability
Phase 3 — Scale the Brand

Goal: become a scalable wellness brand with stronger distribution, systems, and teams.

  • Retail expansion and national distribution
  • Grow product line intentionally
  • Build leadership lanes and reporting structure
  • Add analytics, AI, and automation systems
  • Strengthen brand ecosystem and partnerships

Sprint Control Board

Keep the list tight and only track work that directly matters to the current phase.

Now Finalize MVP flavor shortlist, set up pilot feedback form, confirm first three pilot locations.
Next Draft lean packaging direction and build the initial unit economics model.
Blocked / Risks Ingredient sourcing uncertainty and claims or labeling questions.
Validated / Done Core concept direction confirmed and phase framework defined.
What belongs on the sprint board
  • Only phase-relevant execution items
  • Clearly owned tasks
  • Visible blockers
  • Validated learning, not just busywork
What should not live here
  • Long-term brainstorming
  • Unowned ideas
  • Metrics without action
  • Random admin notes
